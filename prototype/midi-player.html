🎵 Gravestone Melody Player

Prototype companion player — a whole song, carried inside a QR code

The sample above is the exact text inside qr-midi.png — the first phrase of “Amazing Grace.”

How the real visit would work: a visitor scans the small “melody” QR medallion on the stone with their phone. Their scanner shows a block of strange-looking text — that text is the song. They paste it here (in the finished product, one tap in a small companion app), press play, and the hymn plays — with no website, no signal, and no subscription. The music lives in the stone itself.

Why a separate player at all? Ordinary phone scanners can read the text out of a QR code, but they can't turn MIDI numbers into sound. This page is the missing translator: it decodes the text back into a standard MIDI file and plays it with the browser's built-in WebAudio — no server involved. Everything on this page runs on the device.
